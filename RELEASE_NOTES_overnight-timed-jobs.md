# Overnight Jobs

Set a job running before you put the phone down, and come back to collect once it's done — no
taps needed while it runs.

- **New Overnight Jobs screen.** Pick a job, start it, and walk away. Each one takes real time to
  finish — some are done by morning, others take a full day.
- **One job at a time.** While a job is running, it's shown front and center with a live
  countdown. Everything else on the list dims until you collect.
- **Collect when it's ready.** A Collect button lights up the moment the job finishes, paying out
  cash and XP in one tap.
- **Jobs unlock as you level up.** Higher-level jobs pay out more, but need a higher level to
  start.
