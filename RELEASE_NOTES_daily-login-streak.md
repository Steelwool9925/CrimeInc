# Daily Login Streak

A reason to open the app every day. Log in and there's a reward waiting — and the longer your
streak runs, the bigger it gets.

- **A reward every day, automatically offered.** Open the app and if today's reward is ready, it's
  right there waiting to be claimed.
- **Streaks pay off.** Keep logging in day after day and each day's reward grows — miss a day and
  the streak starts over at day one, so don't let it lapse.
- **See your streak at a glance.** Home shows your current streak once you've claimed at least
  once, so you always know where you stand.
- **A new personal best gets called out.** Beat your longest streak yet and the reward screen lets
  you know.
- **Claiming tops off your energy too** — a nice bonus on top of the cash and XP.
