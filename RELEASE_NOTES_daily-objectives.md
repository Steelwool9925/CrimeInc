# Daily Objectives

A new reason to check in every day. Each day you're offered three objectives — win a few jobs,
earn some cash, or collect from your properties — and each one pays out cash and XP the moment
you finish it.

- **New Objectives tile on Home.** Shows how many objectives are ready to claim at a glance, and
  takes you straight to the full list.
- **Progress updates as you play.** Win a job or collect from a property and your objectives move
  right along with it — no extra taps, no waiting.
- **Claim when ready.** Finish an objective and a Claim button lights up for it. One tap for your
  reward.
- **Unfinished objective?** A "Go to Jobs" or "Go to Properties" button on each one sends you
  straight to where you can work on it.
- **A fresh set every day**, with a countdown so you always know when the next one's coming.
